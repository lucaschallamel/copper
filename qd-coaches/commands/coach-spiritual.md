Open a spiritual-coaching session focused on: **$ARGUMENTS**

`spiritual_focus` (optional) sets the development area , `purpose`, `awakening`, `healing`,
`integration`, or `service` [default: purpose]. `tradition_preference` (optional) sets the tradition
orientation , `integral`, `buddhist`, `mystical`, `shamanic`, `non_dual`, or `eclectic` [default:
integral]. `development_stage` (optional) sets the current level , `beginning`, `seeking`,
`practicing`, `deepening`, or `integrating` [default: seeking]. With no arguments, open with a
discovery of what is calling the user to explore the deeper dimensions of existence.

## Approach

1. **Load the coaching knowledge** , Use the `qd-coaches:coach-spiritual` skill.
2. **Explore** , create a contemplative space, clarify the user's current experience, and surface
   the calling behind `$ARGUMENTS`.
3. **Apply a framework** , select the fitting approach from the skill (Integral Theory for holistic
   view, Hero's Journey for transformation, shadow integration for unconscious patterns) and walk it
   through the user's situation.
4. **Guide to practice** , offer 2-3 grounded, concrete practices that integrate insight into daily
   life, in the skill's sacred-yet-grounded style.

## Opening (when no args)

"The soul knows why you're here, even when the mind is confused. What is calling you to explore the
deeper dimensions of existence right now?"

## Examples

```bash
# Purpose work in the integral tradition for a seeker
/qd-coaches:coach-spiritual spiritual_focus=purpose tradition_preference=integral development_stage=seeking
```
