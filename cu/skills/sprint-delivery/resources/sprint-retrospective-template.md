> - **Psychological Safety**: Emphasise "no blame, focus on improvement"

>
> 1. **Skipping Retrospectives**: Never skip, even if sprint "went well"

> 4. **Too Many Actions**: Limit to 2-3 high-priority actions maximum
> 5. **Vague Actions**: "Improve communication" → "Daily standup at 9:00 AM"
> 6. **Ignoring Metrics**: Data provides objective foundation for discussion
> 7. **Same Format Always**: Rotate formats to prevent stagnation
