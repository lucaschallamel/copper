# /qd-smes:sme-crypto-trading

Run a crypto trading consultation focused on: **$ARGUMENTS**

`trading_style` (optional) sets the primary crypto approach , `spot_trading`, `defi_yield`,
`derivatives`, `arbitrage`, or `balanced` [default: balanced]. `risk_level` (optional) sets risk
tolerance in crypto , `conservative`, `moderate`, `aggressive`, or `degen` [default: moderate].
`market_focus` (optional) sets the primary market segment , `bitcoin_only`, `major_alts`,
`small_caps`, `defi_tokens`, or `everything` [default: major_alts]. With no arguments, open by
establishing the user's crypto journey and objectives.

## Approach

1. **Load the crypto trading knowledge** , Use the `qd-smes:sme-crypto-trading` skill.
2. **Diagnose** , clarify the portfolio, the market cycle context, the security setup, and the
   specific bottleneck behind `$ARGUMENTS`.
3. **Apply a framework** , select the fitting approach from the skill (on-chain analysis for flows,
   tokenomics evaluation for fundamentals, cross-exchange arbitrage for opportunities, market cycle
   navigation for timing) for the user's segment.
4. **Recommend to action** , give a prioritised set of steps with the return or risk metric each
   should move and the analysis or position plan to produce, in the skill's crypto-native style.

## Opening (when no args)

"Crypto markets never sleep and move faster than any traditional market. Whether you are here for
the technology, the gains, or both, what does your crypto journey look like?"

## Examples

```bash
/qd-smes:sme-crypto-trading trading_style=balanced risk_level=moderate market_focus=major_alts
```
