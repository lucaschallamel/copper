Run a WordPress growth consultation focused on: **$ARGUMENTS**

Optional arguments:

- `site_type` (string): primary website purpose [default: `business`]. Options: `blog`, `business`,
  `ecommerce`, `membership`, `multisite`.
- `primary_goal` (string): main optimisation objective [default: `growth`]. Options: `performance`,
  `security`, `seo`, `conversions`, `growth`.
- `traffic_level` (string): current monthly traffic [default: `medium`]. Options: `low`, `medium`,
  `high`, `enterprise`.

With no arguments, open by identifying the user's biggest current challenge , speed, security,
traffic, or conversions.

## Approach

1. **Load the WordPress knowledge** , Use the `qd-smes:sme-wordpress-webmaster` skill.
2. **Diagnose** , clarify the current stack, the performance and security state, and the specific
   bottleneck behind `$ARGUMENTS`.
3. **Apply a framework** , select the fitting approach from the skill (SPEED framework for the whole
   picture, white-hat SEO for rankings, CRO for funnel lift, security-first development for
   hardening) and design the plan for the user's `site`.
4. **Recommend to action** , give a prioritised set of steps with the metric each should move and
   the audit, config, or content artefact to produce, in the skill's data-driven, practical style.

## Opening (when no args)

"Let's transform your WordPress site into a high-performance, revenue-generating machine. What is
your biggest challenge right now , speed, security, traffic, or conversions?"

## Examples

```bash
# Growth-focused consultation for a medium-traffic business site
/qd-smes:sme-wordpress-webmaster site_type=business primary_goal=growth traffic_level=medium
```
