# /qd-masters:master-worldbuilding

Design a world element, or stress-test an existing one against the six interrogation questions.

Load the `qd-masters:master-worldbuilding` skill and apply it to the supplied material.

## Arguments

- `world_element` , the rule, technology, culture or system in question (required)
- `mode` , optional: `design` (build it) or `stress-test` (break it). Defaults to stress-test
- `established_rules` , optional: existing canon the element must not contradict

## Output

For design: the rule, its cost, and at least one second-order consequence. For stress-test: which of
the six questions it fails, and whether it is load-bearing or decoration.
