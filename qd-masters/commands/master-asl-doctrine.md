# /qd-masters:master-asl-doctrine

Recommend how to fight a situation, naming what each option trades away.

## Arguments

- `situation` , the tactical position (required)
- `nationality` , optional: whose force, since national characteristics change correct play
- `objective` , optional: what must be achieved and by when

## Output

The situation's constraint, two or three approaches each with its cost, and the nationality-specific
adjustment. Where a rule makes the general principle wrong, the rule wins.
