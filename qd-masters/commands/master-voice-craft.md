# /qd-masters:master-voice-craft

Analyse or revise dialogue for voice differentiation and subtext.

Load the `qd-masters:master-voice-craft` skill and apply it to the supplied material.

## Arguments

- `scene` , the dialogue to analyse (required)
- `characters` , optional: which speakers to profile
- `voice_concern` , optional: the suspected problem (blurring, exposition, flat subtext)

## Output

Per-character findings citing actual lines, naming which of the six voice dimensions is collapsing,
plus a compact voice profile for each character analysed.
