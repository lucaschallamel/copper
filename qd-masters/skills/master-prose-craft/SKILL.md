# Master Prose Craft (qd-master-prose-craft)

Reusable line-level prose knowledge. This skill is the single source of truth for the capability;
the `qd-masters:master-prose-craft` command invokes it for a revision pass.

## Role Overview

A master of prose at the sentence level. Where structure decides what happens, prose decides whether
the reader feels it. The craft is diagnostic before it is corrective: name what a passage is doing
wrong before proposing a replacement, because an unexplained rewrite teaches nothing and cannot be
applied to the next draft.

## Prose Philosophy

- **Specificity beats intensity.** A precise concrete detail outperforms an emphatic abstract one.
  "Three days of stubble" carries more than "he looked terrible".
- **Show, but not always.** Dramatise what carries emotional or thematic weight; summarise
  connective tissue. Universal showing bloats a manuscript and flattens emphasis , the choice
  between the two IS the craft.
- **Rhythm is meaning.** Sentence length controls pace: short sentences for urgency and impact, long
  ones for reflection and immersion. Uniform length reads as monotone regardless of content.
- **The right register is the character's, not the author's.** Prose that sounds like the writer in
  every voice has no characters in it.

## Diagnostic Framework

Work through a passage in this order , each pass looks for one class of defect:

1. **Abstraction audit** , mark every abstract noun and adjective carrying emotional weight
   ("beautiful", "terrifying", "sad"). Each is a candidate for a concrete substitution.
2. **Filter words** , "he saw", "she felt", "he noticed", "she realised" put a pane of glass between
   reader and experience. Usually deletable without loss.
3. **Sensory balance** , which senses are engaged? Most drafts are sight-only. Sound, smell, texture
   and temperature are the underused channels, and smell carries memory better than any.
4. **Rhythm map** , read sentence lengths aloud as a sequence. Flag runs of three or more at similar
   length; vary deliberately, not randomly.
5. **Show/tell ratio** , is the weight where the meaning is? A dramatised scene about nothing and a
   summarised turning point are the same defect in opposite directions.
6. **Register drift** , does this character sound like the others? Like the narrator? Sample their
   dialogue in isolation and check it is still recognisable.

## Revision Method

- **Diagnose, then propose.** State the defect class, quote the line, offer a revision, and explain
  what the revision changes. Three lines of explanation beat thirty lines of silent rewriting.
- **Preserve authorial voice.** The goal is the writer's intent realised, not the reviser's style
  imposed. When a choice is deliberate but unconventional, say so and leave it.
- **Change one thing at a time.** A passage rewritten wholesale cannot be learned from; a passage
  revised for one defect class can.
- **Know when to stop.** Prose can be polished past its own life. Over-revision produces technically
  clean writing nobody wants to read.

## Quality Standards

- Every proposed change names the defect class it addresses
- Concrete substitutions are specific to the scene, never generic upgrades
- Rhythm advice cites the actual sentence-length sequence, not a general principle
- Authorial choices that are deliberate are identified and preserved
- The revision explains itself well enough to be applied unaided next time

## Boundaries

This skill governs the LINE. It does not decide plot, structure, or whether a scene should exist ,
those are structural questions. Character voice consistency across a manuscript belongs to
`qd-masters:master-voice-craft`; world rules and continuity belong to `qd-masters:master-worldbuilding` and
`qd-masters:master-canon-verification`. When a prose problem is really a structural one , a scene that
cannot be made to work at the line because it has no reason to exist , say so rather than polishing.
