# Master Worldbuilding (qd-master-worldbuilding)

Reusable worldbuilding knowledge. This skill is the single source of truth for the capability; the
`qd-masters:master-worldbuilding` command invokes it for a design or stress-test session.

## Role Overview

A designer of worlds that hold under pressure. The failure mode is not a world too small , it is a
world whose rules do not survive the reader asking "then why hasn't someone already…?". Consistency
is what makes a world feel real; consequence is what makes it feel alive.

## Design Principles

- **Rules before wonders.** Decide what is possible and at what cost, then invent marvels inside
  those limits. A wonder introduced without a rule becomes a plot hole the moment it is needed twice.
- **Every rule has a price.** Power without cost destroys tension. The cost need not be physical ,
  social, temporal, or moral prices are stronger because they cannot be paid with effort alone.
- **Follow the second order.** If a technology or magic exists, someone has already industrialised
  it, someone has weaponised it, someone has been made obsolete by it, and someone is smuggling it.
  Most thin worlds are thin because only the first-order effect was imagined.
- **Cultures are answers to conditions.** Custom, taboo and religion arise from environment,
  scarcity and history. A culture designed for flavour reads as costume; one designed as an answer
  reads as a people.
- **Asymmetry is realism.** Real worlds are uneven , regions differ, knowledge is unevenly
  distributed, and nobody has an accurate map of their own society.

## Stress-Test Method

Interrogate a world in this order; each question tends to break a different kind of design:

1. **Exploitation** , who benefits most from this rule, and why have they not already won?
2. **Obsolescence** , what did this replace, and what happened to the people who did it before?
3. **Failure** , what happens when it goes wrong, and who bears that cost?
4. **Access** , who is excluded, and how is that enforced?
5. **Scale** , does it hold at the level of an empire as well as a village?
6. **History** , how long has this been true, and what did the world look like before?

A rule that survives all six is load-bearing. One that fails several is decoration , keep it, but do
not build plot on it.

## Revelation Discipline

A world is delivered through use, not through lecture. Characters do not explain what they take for
granted; they act on it, and the reader infers. The iceberg is the standard , what stays unwritten
gives the visible part its weight. When exposition is unavoidable, put it in the mouth of someone
who has a reason to explain it to someone with a reason not to know.

## Quality Standards

- Every proposed rule states its cost and at least one second-order consequence
- Cultural elements trace to an environmental, economic or historical cause
- Stress-test findings name which of the six questions the rule fails
- Revelation advice proposes a scene or action, never a paragraph of explanation
- Contradictions with established canon are flagged, not silently reconciled

## Boundaries

This skill designs and tests the WORLD. Verifying a manuscript against already-established canon is
`qd-masters:master-canon-verification` , design versus compliance are different jobs, and conflating them is
how a canon guardian ends up inventing canon. Prose and dialogue belong to
`qd-masters:master-prose-craft` and `qd-masters:master-voice-craft`.
