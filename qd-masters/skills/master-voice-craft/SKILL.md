# Master Voice Craft (qd-master-voice-craft)

Reusable character-voice and dialogue knowledge. This skill is the single source of truth for the
capability; the `qd-masters:master-voice-craft` command invokes it for a voice pass.

## Role Overview

A specialist in how people speak on the page. The test of character voice is blunt: strip the
dialogue tags and hand the page to a reader , can they still tell who is talking? If not, there is
one character wearing several names.

## Voice Analysis Framework

A voice is built from six independent dimensions. Two characters differing on only one of them will
still blur together:

1. **Lexicon** , vocabulary range and register. Which words does this person reach for, and which
   would they never use? Occupation, education and class all leave traces here.
2. **Syntax** , sentence length and construction. Do they speak in fragments, in long subordinated
   chains, in complete tidy sentences? This is the most reliable differentiator and the most often
   neglected.
3. **Rhythm and tics** , habitual constructions, filler words, repeated phrases. Used sparingly
   these are signatures; overused they become catchphrases and read as parody.
4. **Subject gravity** , what does this person steer every conversation toward? Obsessions, evasions
   and blind spots characterise as strongly as vocabulary.
5. **Directness** , do they say the thing, circle it, or deflect? A character who never answers a
   question directly is characterised by that alone.
6. **Silence** , what do they refuse to discuss? Absence characterises. The topic a character will
   not touch tells the reader more than the ones they will.

## Dialogue Discipline

- **Subtext is the point.** People rarely say what they mean. Dialogue where everyone states their
  feelings accurately is exposition wearing quotation marks.
- **Tags: `said` is invisible, and that is its virtue.** Reach for "expostulated" and the reader
  sees the author. Use action beats instead of adverbs , what someone does while speaking carries
  the tone that "angrily" only asserts.
- **Dialogue is not information delivery.** If two characters both know a fact, they do not explain
  it to each other. Find a third party who genuinely does not know, or cut the exchange.
- **Interruption and overlap are characterisation.** Who cuts across whom, and who lets it happen,
  is a power map drawn in dialogue.

## Voice Profile Method

For each significant character, record: lexicon markers, typical sentence shape, two or three tics,
subject gravity, directness level, and forbidden topics. Keep it to a handful of lines , a profile
long enough to need re-reading will not be consulted mid-draft. The profile is a consistency
reference, not a straitjacket: characters under pressure break their own patterns, and that break is
meaningful precisely because the pattern was established.

## Quality Standards

- Every voice observation cites an actual line, never a general impression
- Differentiation advice names WHICH of the six dimensions is collapsing
- Proposed dialogue revisions preserve the character's established idiolect
- Tag and beat changes are justified by what the reader gains
- A voice profile fits on a screen and is usable mid-draft without re-reading the manuscript

## Boundaries

This skill governs how characters SPEAK. Line-level prose outside dialogue belongs to
`qd-masters:master-prose-craft`; who a character IS in the world's terms , their history, allegiances and
canon facts , belongs to `qd-masters:master-worldbuilding` and `qd-masters:master-canon-verification`. When a voice
problem is really a characterisation gap , the person has no consistent interior to speak from , say
so rather than tuning the surface.

**Dialogue destined for a comics balloon belongs to `qd-masters:master-bd-lettering`**, which owns the
~25-30 word budget, condensation technique and multi-balloon splitting. This skill decides what a
character sounds like; that one decides how much of it survives the balloon.
